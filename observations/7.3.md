# Observations: 7.3

Desktop system (Intel Core i7-12700K, 32 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 3080 Ti with
the open kernel modules; btrfs RAID1 on four HDDs behind bcache on an NVMe
mdraid1, scx_lavd as sched_ext scheduler). Compared with 6.18.60 and its
series.

## 2026-11-07: 7.3.3 with bcache and btrfs

State: gentoo-sources 7.3.3 with genpatches 7.3-4, `9000_bcache-updates`
(4 patches, tip 2aa2cf63da94) and `9000_btrfs-patches` (19 patches, tip
8f01b7cd4162). Read policy `queue-adaptive`, allocator hints unchanged.

- **Boot time:** kernel 3.9 s, initrd 4.7 s, against 3.96 to 3.99 s and
  4.9 s for the last three 6.18 boots. Userspace took 21.7 s, 10.8 s of it
  `ldconfig.service`, which only runs once after updates.
- **Less sys% in htop, but a higher load average.** Noticed for btrfs in
  general and during a kernel build. Measured after 6.5 h: load 2.3 / 6.5 /
  7.3, CPU pressure about 0.8 %, I/O pressure `some` 30 % and `full` 27 %
  (avg300), up to 4 blocked tasks. The load is mostly tasks waiting for I/O,
  not CPU. Guess: btrfs needs less CPU per I/O since 7.1/7.2 (large folios
  by default, unserialized direct I/O writes, lockless extent map
  counting), so more I/O is in flight and waiting on the slow HDDs.
- **Dolphin lists folders without a blocking pause** of several seconds, as
  seen with 6.18. Possibly a warm page cache after the build (21 GiB
  cached); see the entry after the next reboot.
- **Memory fragmentation** after 21 min: compaction 7 stalls, all
  successful; THP 514 allocations, no fallback; no NVIDIA allocation
  failures. Free blocks of 2 MiB and more: about 58 MiB of 1 GiB free, with
  21 GiB in the page cache. Too early to compare with the failures of
  2026-10-24.
- md127 (the bcache cache device) logs a new 7.3 warning to set
  `md/logical_block_size`; setting it makes the array unusable with
  kernels up to 6.18, so it stays unset while 6.18 is the fallback.

## 2026-11-07: 7.3.3 with platform

State: as above plus `9100_platform` (23 patches, without the BBRv3 default
fix).

- **uksmd works again** with the renumbered process_ksm syscalls (473 to
  475); 213 pages shared 5 min after boot.
- **TCP congestion control was `cubic`**, not BBR: BBRv3 is a separate
  `bbr3` algorithm, and its Kconfig had no default for `DEFAULT_BBR3`.
  Fixed on the branch in 0bc8ca73f8b6; until a rebuild,
  `sysctl net.ipv4.tcp_congestion_control=bbr3` switches at runtime.
- **Dolphin still opens folders without a pause** with a cold cache after
  the reboot, so a warm cache does not explain it. Guess: the btrfs
  metadata path of 7.1/7.2, or fewer reads from the slow HDD of the pool.
  Check with `iostat -x 1` while opening Dolphin after dropping caches.
- **The Steam tray menu reacts immediately.** Open whether this started with
  7.3 or with platform. Candidates: the 7.3 group scheduling rework (test
  with `cgroup_mode=smp`) or the platform patch for `sched_yield` storms.
- **scx_lavd failed at 17:56.** When Chrome started many threads,
  `scx_alloc failed` in `sdt_task.bpf.c` made a `fork` fail; Chrome aborted
  on the failed `pthread_create` (SIGILL), lavd was disabled and its
  userspace part crashed writing to an unmapped arena page (SIGSEGV). Four
  restarts of lavd failed the same way, two of them already while enabling
  the scheduler. Not seen in the 6.5 h of the boot without platform, nor in
  30 days of 6.18; but Chrome never ran in the first boot either, so that
  boot is no comparison.
- **lavd tests at 18:00** (no build running): lavd started again without
  Chrome (1874 threads), stayed enabled with Chrome and a video (3143),
  with 2000 extra sleeping threads (5207) and through 200,000 short-lived
  processes. Neither the number of tasks nor fork churn alone triggers it.
  Likely cause, from the code: since b8467290edab (v7.0-rc1) the BPF arena
  allocates pages under a spinlock with interrupts off, so
  `bpf_map_alloc_pages()` uses `alloc_pages_nolock()` without reclaim (in
  6.18 it ran under a mutex with `GFP_KERNEL`). At 17:56 the kernel build
  and bees left little free memory while Chrome created threads. Not
  confirmed yet; check with `stress-ng --vm` plus fork churn, mitigation
  `vm.watermark_scale_factor`, and a report to scx (lavd could use the
  sleepable allocation of f211c81ddc36, v7.2).
- **18:15: the Dolphin hangs are back.** The folder contents appear, then
  Dolphin is unresponsive for several seconds while the file and folder
  previews load. Still better than with 6.18: the listing shows up at once,
  where 6.18 often sat on an empty window and filled it with folders and
  files in batches. At that moment: I/O pressure `full` 64 % (avg10), CPU
  pressure 0, load 8.9, bees running and the kernel rebuild in progress,
  lavd disabled (EEVDF). The previews read from the HDDs and queue behind
  bees and the build; the scheduler likely plays no part with no CPU
  pressure. Check: pause bees and open a folder with previews; compare
  again with ADIOS after the gaming step.
- scx issue [#3198][scx-3198] (lavd failing with "cannot move migration
  disabled", also triggered by Chrome) is a different error; it was fixed
  by 0c4a59df370b, which 7.3 contains.

[scx-3198]: https://github.com/sched-ext/scx/issues/3198
